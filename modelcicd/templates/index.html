What this is

A self-hosted tool that continuously discovers, benchmarks, and lets you approve which LLM your application actually uses — separately, for each specific feature that calls one. It doesn't look for "the best model" in general; it finds the best model for one job, tested against that job's own test cases and scored against a rubric written for that job.

Your integration point, in full
from modelcicd.resolver import resolve
model = resolve("support_bot_reply", fallback="gpt-4o-mini")

Why it exists

  • New, cheaper, or better models ship every few weeks — nobody manually re-checks, so most teams either overpay or never find out something better exists.
  • A quality regression from a model swap is usually invisible until a user hits it.
  • "Why are we using this model" is usually answered from memory, not evidence.
  • A silent model swap in production should require the same sign-off a dependency upgrade would get.

What it gives you

Lower cost, not guesswork

Every candidate is filtered against your price ceiling before a cent is spent, and ranked only within its own price tier.

Quality protected too

Nothing is proposed without first being scored, blind, against the rubric that feature's own owner wrote.

A record that outlives memory

Every run, candidate, score, and approval is saved with a timestamp and the judge's stated reasoning.

One decision point

A human sees the evidence and clicks Approve. Nothing about production changes silently.

Getting started

  1. Connect an application — optionally with its repo, for scanning and later patching.
  2. Find its AI features — scan the repo, or add one by hand through a guided form. No YAML to write.
  3. Run the bench — the only step that spends money, and it shows the cost and asks first.
  4. Review and approve — the leaderboard shows every candidate's real answer and score. Nothing switches on its own.
